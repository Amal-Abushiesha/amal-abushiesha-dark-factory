How to read the type

Larger, heavier words carry stronger sentiment. Green with a single underline marks positive sentiment; burgundy with a double underline marks negative sentiment. Wider letter spacing signals surprise. A softer italic signals a hedge; upright, full-opacity type is the default, including confident language. The words remain readable in their natural order with or without these visual cues.
